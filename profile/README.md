# King of Gores

King of Gores (KoG) is a community and service provider for **Gores**, the cooperative mode of
[Teeworlds](https://www.teeworlds.com/) / [DDNet](https://ddnet.org/): teams of players solve maps
together, and we keep the servers, the accounts, the rankings and the website running.

## Play and read

- **Website and rankings:** [kog.tw](https://kog.tw)
- **Documentation and community:** [community.kog.tw](https://community.kog.tw)
- **Brand and media:** [brand_and_media](https://github.com/KoG-teeworlds/brand_and_media)
- **Maps:** [maps](https://github.com/KoG-teeworlds/maps)

## What is open here

Most of our platform (the game server, the website, the services behind it and the infrastructure
that runs it) lives in private repositories. What is public is meant to be used and reused:

| Repository | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [community](https://github.com/KoG-teeworlds/community) | Documentation, rules and community pages |
| [maps](https://github.com/KoG-teeworlds/maps) | Maps of the KoG servers |
| [maps-workflow](https://github.com/KoG-teeworlds/maps-workflow) | How maps are built and published |
| [teehistorian-py](https://github.com/KoG-teeworlds/teehistorian-py) | A Python reader for teehistorian files |
| [brand_and_media](https://github.com/KoG-teeworlds/brand_and_media) | Logos and media |

## Test environments

New versions of our platform are tried on a **development** and a **canary** environment before
they reach everybody. They are open to players who log in and accept the terms of that
environment, and they can crash or lose results. Announcements are on the
[community site](https://community.kog.tw).

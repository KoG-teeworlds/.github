# Contributing

Thank you for wanting to help. Some repositories of the organisation are private; the public ones
take contributions as follows.

- **Questions and ideas:** open an issue in the repository, or ask on the
  [community site](https://community.kog.tw).
- **Bugs:** open an issue with what you did, what you expected and what happened.
- **Changes:** fork, make the change on a branch, and open a pull request against `main` with a
  description of what and why. Small, focused pull requests are read first.
- **Maps:** follow the instructions in the [maps](https://github.com/KoG-teeworlds/maps) repository.
- **Security problems:** do not open an issue, see [SECURITY](SECURITY.md).

By contributing you agree that your contribution is under the license of the repository it goes
to. Be kind: we expect everyone to follow the code of conduct of the
[community](https://github.com/KoG-teeworlds/community) repository.
